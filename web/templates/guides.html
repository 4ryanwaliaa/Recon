After a Data Breach: Action Plan

Your data was breached. Don't panic — follow this step-by-step action plan.

  • 01. Change the compromised password immediately
  • 02. Change that password everywhere else you used it
  • 03. Enable 2FA on all critical accounts
  • 04. Check for unauthorized logins and transactions
  • 05. Consider a credit freeze if financial data was exposed
