Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

At RECON OSINT ("we," "our," or "us"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

Search Queries: When you use our OSINT scanner, you submit usernames, email addresses, or names as search queries. These queries are processed in real-time and are not stored on our servers after the scan completes.

Scan Results: Results generated by our scanner are delivered directly to your browser via Server-Sent Events (SSE). We do not retain copies of scan results.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage data such as page views, browser type, and device information to improve our service. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Cookies: We use essential cookies for site functionality. Third-party cookies may be set by Google AdSense for advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our OSINT scanning service
  • To improve and optimize our platform's performance
  • To serve relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • To analyze usage patterns and improve user experience

3. Data Sources

RECON only accesses publicly available information. We query public APIs, public profiles, and publicly accessible databases. We do not access private accounts, protected data, or information behind authentication barriers.

Breach data is provided by third-party services such as LeakCheck and is used solely to inform users about their exposure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense — For displaying advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • LeakCheck — For breach intelligence data. See LeakCheck's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Custom Search API — For Google dorking functionality.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain your search queries or scan results. Anonymous usage analytics may be retained for up to 12 months for service improvement purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Know what data we collect about you
  • Request deletion of any data we hold
  • Opt out of advertising cookies via your browser settings
  • Contact us with any privacy-related concerns

7. Children's Privacy

RECON is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us
