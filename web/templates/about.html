ℹ️ ABOUT US

Built for Digital Awareness

RECON is a free, open-source OSINT intelligence platform designed to help individuals understand and manage their digital footprint. We believe everyone deserves to know what the internet knows about them.

🎯 Our Mission

To democratize digital intelligence and empower individuals to take control of their online presence. In an age where data breaches are commonplace and personal information is constantly being collected, RECON gives you the tools to understand your exposure.

🔍 What We Do

RECON scans your username, email, or name across 120+ platforms including social media, developer sites, gaming platforms, and forums. We also check breach databases and correlate identities to build a comprehensive digital footprint report.

🛡️ Privacy First

We don't store your search queries or results. RECON performs real-time scans against publicly available data — we never access private information or require you to create an account.

⚡ Technology

Built with Python and Flask, RECON uses real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE) for live scan streaming, advanced web scraping for profile enrichment, and identity correlation algorithms to connect the dots.

🌐 Platform Coverage

We scan 120+ platforms including Instagram, Twitter/X, GitHub, Reddit, LinkedIn, Steam, TikTok, Discord, and dozens more. Our email scanner checks 55+ services for account registrations and breach exposure.

📊 Intelligence Features

Beyond simple username checking, RECON provides digital footprint scoring, risk assessment, breach intelligence with exposed field analysis, and identity cluster detection across platforms.
